Overview

Skyline Residence is a bright three-bedroom apartment on the seventh floor of a well-maintained twelve-storey building in the heart of Gulshan 2. Large windows and three balconies bring in plenty of natural light and a pleasant south-east breeze through the day.

The layout separates living and sleeping areas, with a spacious living and dining space, a modern kitchen with a utility area, and a master bedroom with its own dressing room. Schools, hospitals, restaurants and shopping are all within a few minutes' drive.
