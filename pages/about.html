Who we are

Helping families find a place to call home

Nibash is a real estate agency focused on apartments for sale in Dhaka's best residential neighbourhoods. We bring together carefully selected properties, clear information and personal guidance, so every buyer can decide with confidence.

From the first conversation to the day you receive your keys, our team stays beside you and explains each step in plain language.

  • Transparent pricing
  • Legal and paperwork help
  • Support after handover
